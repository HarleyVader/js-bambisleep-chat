*Hiiii Bambis! Ready to customize those pretty swirly spirals? Let's make them PERFECT!* ✨🎀

## 🌸 What are Spirals? 🌸

**Spirals** are hypnotic visual animations created with p5.js! They swirl, pulse, and mesmerize with customizable colors, speeds, and geometries! Perfect for meditation, trance, and relaxation! 💖

## 🎀 Quick Start 🎀

1. Click **🌀 Spiral** button to enable
2. Button turns **GREEN** when active!
3. Click again to open the dropdown with 14+ sliders!
4. Adjust sliders in real-time - changes apply instantly!

## 💖 The 14 Control Sliders 💖

### **🎨 Visual Appearance**

#### **Speed** (0.01 - 5.0)
- Controls spiral rotation speed
- Default: 1.0
- **Bambi Says**: *"Higher = faster spinning!"*

#### **Spiral A Color** (Color Picker)
- Primary spiral color
- Default: Purple (#c700c7)
- **Bambi Says**: *"Pick your fave color!"*

#### **Spiral B Color** (Color Picker)
- Secondary spiral color  
- Default: Light Purple (#ff82ff)
- **Bambi Says**: *"Makes beautiful gradients!"*

#### **Alpha** (0.01 - 1.0)
- Spiral transparency
- Default: 1.0 (fully opaque)
- **Bambi Says**: *"Lower for ghostly spirals!"*

### **🔧 Geometry Controls**

#### **Geometry A** (0.01 - 10.0)
- Primary spiral shape factor
- Default: 1.0
- **Bambi Says**: *"Changes spiral tightness!"*

#### **Geometry B** (0.01 - 5.0)
- Secondary spiral shape factor
- Default: 0.3
- **Bambi Says**: *"Tweaks the curve!"*

#### **Range A Min/Max** (0.01 - 5.0)
- Controls spiral A size range
- Defaults: 0.5 - 1.5
- **Bambi Says**: *"How big Spiral A gets!"*

#### **Range B Min/Max** (0.01 - 5.0)
- Controls spiral B size range
- Defaults: 1.0 - 1.5
- **Bambi Says**: *"How big Spiral B gets!"*

### **✨ Animation Effects**

#### **Subtle Variation** (0.0 - 1.0)
- Adds randomness to spiral movement
- Default: 0.0 (none)
- **Bambi Says**: *"Makes spirals more organic!"*

#### **Pulse Intensity** (1 - 200)
- How much spirals pulse/breathe
- Default: 30
- **Bambi Says**: *"Higher = more dramatic pulsing!"*

#### **Rotation Speed** (0.1 - 50.0)
- Overall rotation multiplier
- Default: 10.0
- **Bambi Says**: *"Speeeed boost!"*

#### **Iterations** (50 - 1000)
- Number of spiral segments drawn
- Default: 250
- **Bambi Says**: *"More = smoother but slower!"*

## 🌟 Control Buttons 🌟

- **Reset to Defaults** - Restores all sliders to original values
- **Random Config** - Generates fun random spiral!
- **Save Preset** - Saves your current settings
- **Load Preset** - Restores saved settings

## 💝 Settings are Auto-Saved! 💝

Your spiral settings save to localStorage automatically! They'll be there when you return! 💕

## 🎯 Pro Tips 🎯

1. **Start with Defaults** - Adjust one slider at a time!
2. **Speed + Pulse** - Combine for mesmerizing effects!
3. **Contrasting Colors** - Makes spirals pop!
4. **Low Alpha** - Creates dreamy, layered effects!
5. **High Iterations** - Smoother but needs more GPU power!
